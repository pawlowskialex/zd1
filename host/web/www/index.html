Connect

    

      
Plug the speaker into this computer with a USB cable and connect, or reach it over your network
         once it runs our software. The page talks to the speaker itself: no account, no cloud.

      
This browser has no WebUSB: open the page in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera.

      

        pick “Zeppelin Air”, or its bootloader, in the browser's list

      
Over the network

      

        
        
        
      

      
The speaker's address on your network (the Network panel shows it once connected over USB). The
         browser asks once to let this page reach devices on your network; the speaker asks for a press of its ON/OFF
         key the first time, and knows this browser from then on. Over the network the page does all but install the
         main board's firmware and the module's boot image: those go over USB (the main board follows its system by
         itself).

      
What is the speaker doing?

      

        Its light shows what it plays: red breathing as it starts, dim red in standby, white for the network, sky blue for USB
        
It runs our firmware: connect. Changes need a press of its ON/OFF key the first time (its light flashes
           purple): a web page can't press it.

      

      

        It still runs B&W's own software
        
B&W's firmware doesn't talk to this page and never shows up in the list. Start it in its bootloader
           instead: unplug its power, hold ON/OFF, plug the power in while holding it, then connect and pick the
           bootloader (03EB:2FF8). The page offers the install from there.

      

      

        A Zeppelin Air with Lightning on B&W's software: its first install, over the network
        
The Lightning model takes its first install over a network cable, through the updater its network module
           starts on its own (both chips from one file). Afterwards it is reachable over the network as above.

        
    
          
  1. Connect it to your router with an Ethernet cable.
    2. 
          
  2. Unplug its power, hold ON/OFF, plug the power in while holding it: its light goes white, and its updater
             takes an address from your router (the router's list of devices shows it).
    3. 
          
  3. Its address here, then Install: the page sends the release's file to it without seeing its answers (it
             doesn't let web pages read them), then waits for our software to answer at the same address.
    4. 
        

        

          
          
          
        

        
The way back is B&W's own file the same way (blobs/zd1l/zd1lv20435.fw in the repository).
           If nothing answers within 15 minutes the speaker refused the file or took another address: its light and
           the router's list tell which.

      

      

        It stopped during an update, or it doesn't start
        
It waits in its bootloader: connect and pick the bootloader. The page shows how far its firmware got and
           offers a repair or a backup this browser kept. If it isn't listed, hold ON/OFF while plugging its power in.

      

      

        It doesn't appear in the browser's list
        
    
          
  • Use a USB cable that carries data (some only charge), straight to the computer.
    • 
          
  • The browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera, on a desktop or on Android. Safari and Firefox have no WebUSB.
    • 
          
  • Windows: our firmware brings its own driver setting (WinUSB). B&W's bootloader doesn't: for it (the speaker
              in DFU, "AT32UC3A" in the Device Manager), install WinUSB once with Zadig
              (Options, List All Devices). Our firmware of a release before this one wants the same for its "Interface 3"
              only, never the whole speaker: sound and keys keep Windows' own drivers.
    • 
          
  • Linux: give your user the device with this rule, then plug the speaker in again:
            
    sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/70-zd1.rules <<'EOF'
SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTR{idVendor}=="03eb", ATTR{idProduct}=="2fe0", TAG+="uaccess"
SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTR{idVendor}=="03eb", ATTR{idProduct}=="2ff8", TAG+="uaccess"
EOF
sudo udevadm control --reload-rules

             goes to the speaker.

        

        
The speaker's radio is 802.11b/g: 2.4 GHz networks only. WPA2 and WPA2/WPA3 networks join;
           WPA3-only networks can't (its driver has no WPA3).
